## Framing rules
- 1930s timeline is shot in the **1.37:1 Academy ratio**. The chapter uses a 1.37:1 frame, pillarboxed/letterboxed on other screens.
- One-point perspective with the vanishing point on the exact vertical center line.
- Architecture carries the symmetry: paired elevators, twin staircases, arched openings, a centered desk or counter.
- Lots of headroom above subjects; chandeliers and signs anchor the top of the frame.

## Rhythm & camera
- Lateral dollies ("whip" or slow track) that stop dead on a new centered tableau. Each stop is a composed frame.
- Pans hold, move at constant speed, hold again. No drift, no handheld feel.
- → Implementation: pan = hold / linear-ish move / hold, one room per stop.

## Typography
- Engraved, formal lettering on signage; condensed display caps; gold on plum.
- Chapter cards: centered small caps + large numeral, framed with a thin rule.
