## Framing rules
- Flat, elevation-drawing façades: buildings shot dead-on like a stage set, stacked in depth planes.
- The film switches between color and black & white. The switch is a **cut**, used as punctuation.
- Magazine-page compositions: masthead, rules, columns, captioned illustrations.

## Rhythm & camera
- Vertical crane/tilts reveal a building floor by floor; cutaway set dollies.
- Plenty of tableau vivant: everything frozen while the camera moves.
- → Implementation: vertical tilt up a layered street (parallax per plane), then hard cut to a B&W magazine spread.

## Typography
- Mid-century magazine: condensed serif body text, big geometric masthead, red rules.
- → Masthead in Jost, body in Libre Caslon Text.
- All names (magazine, town) are invented; nothing from the film.
