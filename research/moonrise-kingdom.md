# Moonrise Kingdom (2012) — style research

## Final palette
| Token | Hex | Role | Change vs brief |
|---|---|---|---|
| `--mk-khaki` | `#C2B280` | Scout uniform, tiles | kept |
| `--mk-forest` | `#3E5641` | Table / ground | kept |
| `--mk-mustard` | `#D4A017` | Tent canvas, key accent | kept |
| `--mk-rust` | `#A0522D` | Leather, badges | kept |
| `--mk-sky` | `#9FB8C8` | Water, maps | kept |
| `--mk-paper` | `#EDE3C4` | Labels, letters, text on forest (~7:1) | added |
| `--mk-ink` | `#2A2A22` | Typewriter ink | added |

## Framing rules
- Top-down inserts of belongings laid out on a flat surface in a strict grid (the "packing list" shot).
- Each object is labeled, catalogued, numbered — a field-guide / inventory aesthetic.
- Warm, yellowed daylight; everything feels printed on 1960s film stock.

## Rhythm & camera
- Insert shots are held static; reveals happen by cutting in new objects, not moving the camera.
- Pans are strict 90° swings or straight tracks along a camp line.
- → Implementation: static overhead frame; objects appear one step at a time in mirror-symmetric pairs so every intermediate frame is still symmetric.

## Typography
- Hand-painted and typewritten labels; stencil and embroidered badge lettering.
- → Titles in geometric caps (Jost), labels in a typewriter face (Courier Prime).
